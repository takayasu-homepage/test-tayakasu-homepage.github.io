Josui-Kaikan, Hitotsubashi University

Access to “Josui-Kaikan”, Hitotsubashi University (Address: 2-1-1, Hitotsubashi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo)
3-4 minutes walk from either “Jimbocho” Station (Exit A8) or “Takebashi” Station (Exit 1B) (see Map Josui-Kaikan).

Address 2-1-1, Hitotsubashi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
Major Routes of Access

From Narita Airport
Take a Narita skyliner (Keisei Line) to “Nippori” Station and transfer to JR Yamanote Line.
Take a train bound for Shinjuku (counter-clockwise) to “Sugamo” Station.
At “Sugamo”, transfer to Mita Line (Toei Subway) and get off at “Jimbocho” subway Station.
The whole journey takes about 80 minutes.

Three subway lines are crossing at “Jimbocho” station, Hanzomon Line, Mita Line and Shinjuku Line.
Mita Line is connecting “Ookayama” Station directly.
The ride from Ookayama Station to Jimbocho Station takes about 30 minutes.

West Bldg. 9, Ookayama Campus, Tokyo Institute of Technology

Access to “West Bldg. #9”, Tokyo Institute of Technology (Address: Ookayama, Meguro-ku, Tokyo)
West Bldg. #9 in Ookayama campus is a few minutes walk from “Ookayama” Station. (see Map Ookayama)

Address 2-12-1, Ookayama, Meguro-ku, Tokyo
Major Routes of Access
From Downtown Tokyo to Ookayama Station there are 4 ways
1: Take Keihin-Tohoku Line(Japan Railways) to Ooimachi Station.
Transfer to Ooimachi Line (Tokyu Transportation Systems) there and get off at Ookayama Station. The train ride from Ooimachi Station to Ookayama Station is about 10 minutes.
2: Take Yamanote Line (Japan Railways) to Meguro Station.
Transfer to Meguro Line (Tokyu Transportation Systems) there and get off at Ookayama Station. The ride from Meguro Station to Ookayama Station takes about 10 minutes.
3: Take Mita Line (Toei Subway) to Meguro Station and continue the ride to Ookayama Station.
4: Take Namboku Line (Tokyo Metropolitan Subway) to Meguro and continue the ride to Ookayama.
General information from Narita Airport to Downtown Tokyo
1: Take a Narita skyliner railway to Nippori Station or Ueno Station.
2: Take a Narita express (JR railway) to Tokyo Station, or Shinagawa Station or Shinjuku Station.
3: Take a limousine bus runs from the airport to various destinations
4: Take a taxi (very expensive, about 20,000 yen).
