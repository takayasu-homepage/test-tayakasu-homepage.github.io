The abstract/paper submission process has several steps: (1) submission of a
preliminary, initial abstract, by October 31, 2008, (2) notification of
abstract acceptance, by November 28, 2008, and (3) submission of final, paper, in
print ready form, by March 31, 2009.

\vspace{12pt}

The final abstract length should not exceed 2 pages. The final abstract must
contain (1) the presentation title, (2) names of the presentation authors,
and (3) contact information for the corresponding author, including authors'
postal and e-mail addresses. Templates for the preparation of the abstracts
in LaTeX and MS Word may be found on the THIC \& APFA7 Conference website
(http://www.thic-apfa7.com/). Once the abstract is prepared please
convert the abstract to PDF format. All abstracts (PDF files) must
be submitted in electronic form via the website. 

\vspace{12pt}
